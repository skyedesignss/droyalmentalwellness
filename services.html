Two Paths of Support

At Droyal Mental Wellness we provide focused care through two main programs. Both are delivered in a professional outpatient setting and are tailored to the goals of the individuals and families we serve.

Whether you are looking for therapy and clinical support or practical help building daily living skills, our team is here to guide you with clarity and respect.

Outpatient Mental Health Clinic

Clinical care for children and adults

Our Outpatient Mental Health Clinic (OMHC) provides assessment, therapy, and psychiatric support in a professional setting. Services are tailored to each person’s goals and delivered with clear communication and respect.

We work with children, adolescents, and adults. Care may include individual sessions, family involvement, group therapy, and medication management when appropriate.

Request an Appointment

What OMHC includes

  • Individual Therapy One-on-one sessions focused on personal goals and challenges.
  • Family Therapy Support that includes family members when it benefits the person receiving care.
  • Group Therapy Structured group sessions that foster connection and shared learning.
  • Psychiatric Evaluation Comprehensive assessment to understand needs and guide treatment.
  • Medication Management Ongoing support and monitoring when medication is part of the plan.
  • Treatment Planning & Care Coordination Clear plans and coordination with other providers as needed.

Psychiatric Rehabilitation Program

Practical support for greater independence

Our Psychiatric Rehabilitation Program (PRP) helps adults build the daily living skills, confidence, and community connections needed for greater stability and independence.

PRP is skills-focused and highly practical. We work alongside each person to strengthen the areas that matter most in everyday life, from managing responsibilities to engaging with the community.

Request an Appointment

What PRP includes

  • Daily Living Skills Support with routines, personal care, and everyday responsibilities.
  • Employment Support Help preparing for, finding, and maintaining meaningful work.
  • Money Management Practical guidance around budgeting and financial responsibilities.
  • Social & Community Integration Building connections and participating more fully in community life.
  • Wellness & Recovery Tools and habits that support long-term mental health and stability.
  • Housing Stability & Resource Linkage Assistance with housing-related goals and connecting to community resources.

Who These Services Are For

Support matched to your situation

Both programs are designed for real people with real goals. Here is who each service line typically serves.

OMHC is for

  • Children and adolescents needing therapy or evaluation
  • Adults seeking individual or family therapy
  • Individuals who may benefit from psychiatric evaluation or medication management
  • Families who want to be involved in the care process

PRP is for

  • Adults working toward greater daily independence
  • Individuals who want practical support with living skills
  • People seeking help with employment, money management, or community connections
  • Those who need linkage to housing or other community resources

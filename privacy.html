1. Introduction

Droyal Mental Wellness (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of the individuals and families we serve. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

Because we provide mental health and psychiatric rehabilitation services, we take additional care with sensitive information. Some information may also be protected under applicable state and federal laws, including laws governing protected health information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information : such as name, phone number, email address, and mailing address when you request an appointment, submit a form, or contact us.
  • Appointment and service-related information : details you choose to share when requesting care or completing intake-related forms.
  • Technical and usage information : such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, which may be collected automatically when you use our website.
  • Communications : messages you send to us through forms, email, or phone.

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your requests, provide services, and operate our website.

3. How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to appointment requests, inquiries, and referrals
  • Coordinate care and provide mental health or psychiatric rehabilitation services
  • Communicate with you about your requests or services
  • Improve our website, forms, and user experience
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our systems
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in limited circumstances, such as:

  • With your consent or at your direction
  • With service providers who assist us in operating our website or supporting our operations (under appropriate agreements)
  • When required by law, court order, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of our organization, our clients, or others, when permitted or required by law

Clinical and health-related information is handled with heightened care and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality, understand how the site is used, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some parts of the site function.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13 for independent online use. When we provide services involving minors, information is collected and handled in accordance with applicable law and with appropriate parent or guardian involvement as required.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as requesting access, correction, or information about how your data is used. To make a request or ask a privacy-related question, please contact us using the information below.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (for example, video platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
